Hi there! I'm Weiyan (石唯妍) WAY-yen, a Ph.D. candidate at Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) and a member of the Context-Aware Interaction Lab, working on human–computer interaction. I’ll soon be joining TikTok as a research intern.

My research explores how people and AI can communicate and collaborate on real-world tasks across design, child development, and community safety. I’m particularly interested in how AI can support professional practices where people draw on their experience to make sense of complex situations and decide how to act.

Previously, I was a research intern at A*STAR. I also interned at DiDi as a machine learning engineer and at Kuaishou as a software engineer. I received my master’s degree from Peking University and my bachelor’s degree from Beijing Institute of Technology.

Around the internet, I’m @missckroach ✦
