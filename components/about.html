Hi there! I'm Weiyan WAY-yen, a Ph.D. student at Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD).

When people design, the final idea is only part of the story — sketches, revisions, and changing intentions reveal how the idea takes shape. I study whether AI can align not only with what people produce, but with the processes through which they get there.

I build with multimodal large language models and agentic AI, and develop prototypes and evaluation methods that represent human processes, steer AI reasoning with them, and measure how well the two align.
