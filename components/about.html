Hi there! I'm Weiyan WAY-yen, a Ph.D. candidate at Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) and a member of the Context-Aware Interaction Lab, working toward becoming an AI anthropologist.

My research focuses on human-AI alignment, which I see as necessary for future human-centred AI. I ask "whether AI can learn from how humans actually work", especially in multimodal practices such as interpreting behaviour in videos and developing ideas through sketching. I explore this question using large language model and agentic AI, grounded in real professionals in these domains rather than static datasets.
